What works in this build

Captions are written offline on your phone (English, and Hindi/Urdu speech). Clips are rendered by the phone’s hardware video encoder and saved straight to your Gallery.

Finding moments

Scores each second from audio loudness, loudness changes and on-screen motion, then picks the strongest non-overlapping windows. This is signal-based ranking, not scene understanding.

Captions

With the key, captions understand any spoken language (Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, English and 90+ more) with exact word timing. Without it, the app uses its offline English and Hindi/Urdu models.

Internet needed, only when you tap “Title and rate with AI” (or run Auto Create with a key). Only the caption text of your clips is sent to Google. No video and no audio is ever uploaded. Ratings describe how strong an opening is. They do not predict views.

Auto-captions run offline on your phone and time every word. English and Hindi/Urdu speech are built in (Urdu speech is written in Devanagari script). For other languages, import an .srt file. Check the lines in the editor.
